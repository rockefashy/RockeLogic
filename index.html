Case study 01 · SmartPen Academy

SmartPen Academy
Handwriting Coaching Application

An established handwriting coaching academy with multiple batches, coaches, and students needed to eliminate spreadsheet chaos and manual coordination by building a centralized operational web portal.

RockeLogic engineered the complete coaching application: custom modules to create and manage students and coaches, track attendance, manage fee collection via Razorpay, record weekly progress, and trigger automated WhatsApp notifications and reminders.

Students & Coaches Hub Attendance Tracking Fee Management & Razorpay Weekly Progress Tracking Automated WhatsApp Alerts

The solution

A bespoke coaching application to create and manage students and coaches, track daily attendance, manage fee collections, record weekly progress evaluations, and dispatch automated WhatsApp reminders and alerts.

The outcomes

Completely replaced manual spreadsheets and messaging confusion with a single source of truth. Automated fee reconciliation with Razorpay. Real-time weekly progress reports visible to parents, coaches, and administrators.
