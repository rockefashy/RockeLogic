Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know
Before Launching

Does my web portal include a built-in SEO framework?

Yes, absolutely. Every portal includes programmatic JSON-LD Schema (Course, Organization, FAQ), XML sitemaps, OpenGraph social preview tags, and lightning-fast Core Web Vitals optimized for Google indexing from Day 1.

Do I need any technical or coding knowledge?

No, zero technical knowledge is needed. RockeLogic handles domain setup, cloud hosting, database architecture, payment integrations, and code maintenance. You receive an intuitive dashboard to manage your content and revenue.

Can you build a portal exactly like SmartPen Academy?

Yes. SmartPen Academy is a live example of our turnkey work. We build customized academies with video protection, student progress tracking, quizzes, automated certificate generation, and instant checkout.

How does company registration work?

We guide you through choosing the right legal entity (Private Limited, LLP, OPC, or Sole Proprietorship), assist with document preparation, file for incorporation, and obtain your GSTIN number so you can legally invoice and accept payments.

Who owns the code, website, and student data?

You own 100% of your business, domain name, source code, student database, and payment accounts. You maintain complete sovereignty over your digital assets with no vendor lock-in.

How long does the entire launch process take?

Our standard turnaround is 30 days from project kickoff to public launch. For founders who already have their company registered, we can often launch the web portal in as little as 2 to 3 weeks.
